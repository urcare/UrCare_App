Delete your UrCare account

This page explains how to delete your UrCare account and the data linked to it.

Option 1: Delete from the app (instant)

  1. Open the UrCare app and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Type DELETE and tap Delete forever.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you can't open the app, email urcarein@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my UrCare account". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your health profile, lab reports, food scans, daily plans and tracker logs
  • Chats with the care team, consultations, prescriptions and notifications
  • Family members you added, and their data
  • Order history and saved addresses

What may be kept

Nothing linked to your account is kept in the app after deletion. Your bank and UPI app keep their own transaction records as required by law.
