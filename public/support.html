UrCare Support

Need help with the UrCare app or website? We usually reply within 1–2 working days.

Contact us

  • Email: urcarein@gmail.com
  • WhatsApp / phone: +91 79831 43151
  • In the app: open the care team chat, or open the menu and tap Give Feedback.

When you write to us, include the email address you use to sign in to UrCare and a short description of the problem. Please don’t send passwords.

Common questions

I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen, tap Sign in, then Forgot password. We’ll email you a reset link.

A lab report or meal scan result looks wrong. AI results can be incomplete or wrong. Tap Report a problem with this AI result under the result, or correct the report text yourself from the report screen, and always check results with your doctor.

I’m not getting reminders. Allow notifications for UrCare in your phone’s settings, and turn on Notifications in the app’s Settings.

Where is my order? Open My Orders in your account page to see its status, or contact us with your order number.

How do I delete my account? In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. You can also follow the steps on our account deletion page. Deleting your account permanently removes your data.

Medical emergencies

UrCare gives general wellness guidance and is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital.
