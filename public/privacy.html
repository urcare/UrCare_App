Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how UrCare ("UrCare", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects your information when you use the UrCare mobile app and website (the "Service"). UrCare is operated from India and handles personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and sign-in details when you register with email, Google or Apple.
  • Health profile: age, gender, height, weight, goals, dietary preferences, medical conditions and other details you enter during onboarding or in your profile.
  • Health documents and logs: lab reports and prescriptions (photos, PDFs or text) you upload, the values extracted from them, meal photos and descriptions you scan, daily plans you upload, and the activity, weight and tracker entries you log.
  • Family members: health details of family members you choose to add and manage from your account.
  • Communications: messages you exchange with our care team and doctors, consultation requests, and feedback you send us.
  • Orders and payments: products ordered, delivery name, address and phone number, the screenshot of your UPI payment that you upload, and the UPI reference number if you enter it. You pay from your own UPI app; we never see or store your UPI PIN, card or bank login details.
  • Technical information: basic device and log information needed to keep the Service secure and working.

We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not collect your precise location.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the Service: build your personalised plan, analyse your reports and meals, show your history, and let the care team and doctors support you.
  • To process orders and consultations, and to verify your payment by checking the payment screenshot you upload.
  • To send you notifications about your account, reports, plans and orders.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent misuse, and fix problems.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We never sell your personal data and never share it with advertisers.

3. AI analysis

When you choose to analyse a lab report, meal or daily plan, the file or text you selected is sent over an encrypted connection to our AI service providers, Anthropic (Claude) and Groq, only to produce the result shown to you. Under their commercial terms these providers do not use this data to train their models. The app asks for your permission before the first time any of your data is sent for AI analysis. AI results are general wellness information, not a medical diagnosis.

4. Service providers we share data with

We share data only with providers that help us run the Service, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • Supabase – secure database, file storage and sign-in.
  • Render – application hosting.
  • Anthropic and Groq – AI analysis (see section 3).
  • Google and Apple – sign-in, if you choose those options.
  • Doctors and care-team members working with UrCare – to provide consultations and care you request.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of any person.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored with access controls so that each user can only reach their own records. Some of our providers may process data on servers outside India; where they do, we rely on their contractual and security safeguards.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, reports, plans, chats, orders and family-member data are permanently deleted from our systems. Your bank and UPI app keep their own transaction records as required by law.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction: view and edit your information in the app at any time.
  • Deletion: delete your account and data from Settings → Delete Account in the app, or follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Withdraw consent: you can stop using AI analysis at any time; already-received results stay in your account until you delete them.
  • Grievances: contact our Grievance Officer using the details below.

8. Children

UrCare accounts are intended for people aged 18 or older. A parent or guardian may add and manage a child as a family member from their own account, and is responsible for the information they add.

9. Not for emergencies

UrCare is not an emergency service. If you have a medical emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital immediately.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will show the new date at the top and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
